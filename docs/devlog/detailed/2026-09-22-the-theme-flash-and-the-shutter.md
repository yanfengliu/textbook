## The theme flash: the shell set the reader's theme after the page had been styled

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-22 22:57 – 23:43 (the landing of the fix and its two checks); 2026-09-23 10:18 – 10:26 (the checks proved again on the files as first handed back for review); 2026-09-23 11:20 – 11:29 (the review's fixes to both checks, proved red again, and every arm re-run on the final files)

**Action:** `src/theme-early.js` exports `THEME_KEY` and `THEME_BLOCK`, a classic inline script that resolves the theme exactly as `src/shell.js` does (`?theme=dark|light` first and never stored, then the stored choice, then nothing), with every storage access inside a `try`. The block was pasted into the `<head>` of the 13 pages that load the shell, before the first stylesheet, each page's CRLF endings kept. The pages were found on disk by `tools/lib/shell-pages.js`, which follows each published page's scripts and their imports to `src/shell.js`, so `lab/` is included. The shell now imports `THEME_KEY` rather than spelling it. Two checks hold it: `test/theme-early.test.js` in `npm run unit`, on the text of every page, and `npm run theme` (`tools/theme.js`), the chain's thirteenth step, in a browser. `docs/design/chapter-recipe.md` tells the next chapter to carry the block.

**Result:** Before, recorded frame by frame on the frozen tree with the system preferring light and dark stored: the whole page in the other theme for one to three frames, then the titles fading across in a 240 ms transition of which about 150 ms was visible, on 6 of 6 loads of Today, 5 of 6 of the biology contents page, 4 of 6 of chapter 1, 2 of 6 of the library and 1 of 6 of the tongjian contents page. The prototype of the page fix on the three contents pages: 0 frames in the other theme and 0 frames of fade in 18 of 18 loads, while Today, left without the block as the control, flashed on 6 of 6. On this tree: `npm run theme` clean over 91 loads, 7 situations on each of 13 pages.

**Reasoning:** The alternative prototyped first was to suppress transitions until the shell had run. It was rejected because the flash itself stayed, on 13 of 24 loads: a transition is only the second half of the defect, and the first half is that the page is styled before any module can run. A module cannot fix that from inside itself, and a page cannot `import` the block, because it has to run before any module could. So every page carries a copy, and a copy is what goes missing on a new page or drifts on an old one; the unit test is the thing that holds the copies, and its failure prints the block and where it goes, because chapters written on other branches will meet it cold. The block sits before the first stylesheet rather than anywhere in `<head>`, because a classic script after a stylesheet still loading waits for it.

**Validation:** Four mutations, each on its own page and each restored to its recorded sha256: the block deleted (the mutated page is byte-identical to that page on `5e9c981`, so this arm is the real defect), the storage key drifted, the `try` removed, and the block moved to the end of `<head>`. The unit test fails all four, naming the page. `npm run theme` fails the first three and passes the fourth, and that green arm is its bound, recorded as one: a block at the end of `<head>` still sets the attribute before `<body>` exists, so only the text check can hold the placement. Neither check sees pixels, WebKit or Gecko, or a flash in the right theme, such as a late stylesheet. Proofs: `docs/learning/gate-proofs.md`.

## `npm run shot` is not byte-reproducible, and four beliefs about why were false

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-17 (the baseline, on the frozen tree) – 2026-09-23 09:32 – 09:41 (the acceptance measurement)

**Action:** The shutter now waits: before each screenshot `tools/shot.js` polls `document.getAnimations()` once per animation frame until nothing is running, and fails a page still moving after 120 frames, naming what moves. `tools/shot.js`'s header says what the frames are for — a person looking at them at native resolution, never a hash — with each remaining class, its mechanism and its size. `AGENTS.md`'s invariant *"This is what makes a screenshot the same frame every run"* was false and now says what is true: a figure's state and drawing are the same every run, the gate's screenshot bytes are not, and a figure's frame may not depend on when a webfont arrived. `soup` breaks that last rule, and is recorded in `docs/learning/defect-register.md` rather than named in the invariant.

**Result:** Over the 12 pages both trees carry — the library, both contents pages, biology chapters 1 to 5, tongjian chapters 1 to 3 and Today, at three widths in two themes, 72 frames — three full untrimmed runs on the frozen 2026-09-17 tree left 39 of 72 frames byte-identical, 33 differing, and 27, 24 and 27 differing per pair. Three on this tree, one after another with the tree held still: **61 of 72 identical, 11 differing, 8 per pair.** The frozen tree also held biology chapters 6 and 7, whose 12 frames were identical in all three runs, so over its own 14 pages it was 51 of 84; the drop from 33 differing frames to 11 is all on the shared pages. All 11 that differ are biology chapters 2 to 5 at tablet or desktop width. Every cluster of differing pixels in them but one was traced to one of three classes: SVG label advances (3 frames, up to 4,250 px and 74/255), `backdrop-filter` blur (6 frames, at most 2/255) and the first figure stage's rounded corners (8 frames, at most 2/255). The one left is a 21-pixel speck at 2/255 on the rounded end of `soup`'s temperature pill, which neither clips nor blurs, and it is recorded as unexplained. The Today frames, the contents pages, the tongjian chapters and every phone frame were identical in all three. The wait costs 1.9 to 2.9 s a full run.

**Reasoning:** What was believed and proved false:

- *"The library class is rounding bounded at 1/255."* False. The library's own frames differed by up to 6/255 on the phone and 27/255 on the desktop, and by 90/255 on this tree with the block taken out. The cause is a colour transition: the titles were photographed mid-fade.
- *"It is the sticky header's blur."* Ruled out. The box that differs is the heading, at y 975 to 2010 on the phone frame, not the header.
- *"The SVG class is a font-arrival race."* False. It is a Chromium font-cache collision: two label sizes 0.01 px apart can share one cache entry, and whichever is shaped first sets both labels' advances. A page with no book code on it reproduces it, and priming one text at one size before the figures mount fixed all six label widths in 5 of 5 runs. The figures' whole-pixel pane boxes feed it, by drawing a label at 0.998 to 1.001 of its size.
- *"The Today frame is stateful because `npm run sitting` rewrites the record."* False, and already disproved on 2026-09-17: the frame was a race between the handshake and `<tb-sitting>`'s boot, closed by the handshake's sitting term. No Today frame differed in the baseline's three runs or in this round's.

**Validation:** The differ that compares frames is a scratch PNG decoder, so its five controls were re-run on this measurement's own frames before its comparison was trusted, and it agreed byte for byte with pngjs on every frame and pair it was given. Every band was classified by what the page has under it: figure SVG text, a `backdrop-filter` element, or a rounded stage corner. The dark contents pages and one dark chapter frame were looked at at native resolution. The settle wait was proved on this tree before the acceptance runs: eight interleaved runs of four arms on the dark contents pages, five of two arms on the tongjian chapters (18 of 18 frames identical with the wait, 10 of 18 differing without it), and an infinite animation that fails naming itself.

**Code reviewer comments:** The same review. Three findings were about this section's claims. The design of record still promised a screenshot that is the same frame every run, in `docs/design/textbook.md` twice, and `tools/lib/browser.js` rested its CPU-rasterizer default on that promise; both now say what is true, and the rasterizer comment rests on the sweep's own measurement (R1). `AGENTS.md`'s invariant named `soup` as a known breach, which is status in a standing rule; the rule stays, and `soup` moved to the defect register with its mechanism, its owner and "no gate yet" (R2). And "61 of 72" had been set against "51 of 84" as before and after, two populations of different size: both are now stated over the 12 pages both trees share, which chapters 6 and 7 did not change, since none of their frames differed (R7). The reviewer checked the settle wait's ordering, its bound and its 400 ms ceiling, and found them sound. A focused re-review of the fixes found them **ready to land**. Its two optional nits are deferred and open: F1, the interval failure's wording in `tools/theme.js`, and F2, a malformed `%` in a `src` making `tools/lib/shell-pages.js` throw an error that names neither the page nor the `src`.
