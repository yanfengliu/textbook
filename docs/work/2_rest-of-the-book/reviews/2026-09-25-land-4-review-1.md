## Findings

### S1 (should-fix). The term-hold does not hold a mark glued to a term by a no-break space, and chapter 8 strands two of them

**Location.** `src/components/term.js:366` (`heldRun`) and its bound at `:350`. The two cases are `biology/ch08-dna/index.html:180` (`phosphodiester bond</tb-term>&nbsp;— a phosphate…`) and `:244` (`deamination</tb-term>&nbsp;— it loses…`). The claim it contradicts is `docs/learning/defect-register.md:851`.

**What is wrong.** `heldRun` holds only closing, final, other and dash punctuation and a run-on word. A U+00A0 no-break space is none of those, so for `&nbsp;—` it returns 0 and `holdAfter` wraps nothing. The no-break space does not help on its own: CSS Text 3 keeps a soft wrap opportunity after an atomic inline "even when adjacent to a character that would normally suppress them, including U+00A0". So the line may break after the term's button, and the next line then begins with the no-break space and the dash. This is the class the defect-register entry of 2026-09-24 fixes, and the book's style writes `&nbsp;—` precisely so that a dash never begins a line.

**Evidence.** A scratch probe (`scratchpad/review-land/nbsp-dash.mjs`, standing in for `npm run inspect`, which frames elements but measures no line break) loaded chapter 8 as staged and swept every width from 320 to 1440 px in steps of 1 px, light theme. It has three arms:

| Arm | What it measures | Result |
|---|---|---|
| real | the page as served | both cases strand. `phosphodiester-bond` at 52 of 1,121 widths (355 to 356, 549 to 556, 670 to 680, 858 to 866, 990 to 999, 1038 to 1049). `deamination` at 41 (320 to 324, 364, 661 to 671, 980 to 991, 1028 to 1039) |
| control | every held case in chapter 8 unwrapped again | 30 of 36 strand at some width, including "pyrimidine dimer" at 390 px and "proofreading" at 320 px, the same two chapter 8 cases the term-comma handoff measured before the fix. So the instrument sees the class |
| fixed | the two cases wrapped by hand in a `<tb-term-hold>` with their no-break space and dash | 0 of 2 strand at any width |

I looked at crops at native size: `deamination` at 320 px in the light theme and `phosphodiester bond` at 355 px in the dark theme and at 670 px in the light theme. Each shows a line that begins with a one-space indent and a dash (" — it loses its amino group…", " — a phosphate joining…").

**Why the landing's measurement did not see it.** The register row at `defect-register.md:851` and the handoff both say 0 stranded "at every width from 320 to 1440 px, in biology ch01–08". Two things put these cases outside that measurement:

- The probe that measured it, `strand.mjs`, counts a term as glued only when the next character fails JavaScript's `/\s/`. That class includes U+00A0, so the probe could not count a no-break space followed by a mark, at any width. That is a bound it did not name.
- Chapter 8's text changed after the measurement. The handoff says ch07 and ch08 were measured in their own worktrees "at about 09:20". Both `&nbsp;—` joins are on `origin/ch08`, which land-4 merged in `b376f01` (from `b542ea1`).

I checked every `</tb-term>` in both books at `f1ae404`. These two are the only terms followed by a no-break space, in any spelling (`&nbsp;`, `&#160;`, a literal U+00A0, U+202F or U+2060). On every remote branch, `</tb-term>&nbsp;` occurs only in these two, and both are still there on `origin/figfix-ch08` (`2f43ef8`). No biology term has a mark glued before it or a tag directly after it.

**Fix.** Two parts:

- In `heldRun`, treat a no-break space (U+00A0, U+202F, and U+2060 for completeness) as glue: hold it, and hold the next character too, a mark or the first letter of a word. Then continue the run as now. Add both chapter 8 shapes to `test/term-hold.test.js` and prove the new case red. `term.js` is the right place for the fix, not the prose: `&nbsp;—` is the house style.
- Correct `defect-register.md:851`. Re-measure chapters 7 and 8 at their final text with a probe that counts a no-break space as glue, and state the claim with that bound. The fixed arm above is that probe's expected result.

`term.js` is outside what the later branches change, so this pass is the only one that covers it.
