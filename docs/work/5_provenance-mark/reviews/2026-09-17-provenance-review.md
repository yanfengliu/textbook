# Independent review — the provenance change, 2026-09-17

Read-only. Nothing in the repository was edited; the mutation tests it ran were on a copy under `%TEMP%`. The reviewer was a separate agent with no part in writing the change, given the diff, the design of record, `test/lexicon.test.js`, `tongjian/corpus.js`, `tongjian/data/card.js` and `src/components/term.js`, and asked to be adversarial: to find a way the gate proves less than its header claims, to find strings the two predicates misclassify, and to check the CSS against the real DOM.

Its green baseline: `test/provenance.test.js` passed (3 pages, 8 原文 blocks, 48 marked runs, 48 prose quotations, 5 declared fields, 10 figure quotations) and `test/lexicon.test.js` passed.

## Kept as the reviewer wrote it

**Verdict: not ready as is** — one real defect in the pages, two reproduced gate blind spots, one header overclaim; the CSS and the figure edits verified sound.

1. **「不可」 marked as 通鑑's own words, though the note beside it says the received text does not contain them.** Chapter 2's `data-note="textual"` aside reads 「不可」二字不见于底本，是整理者依十二行本等本子补入的 — and `corpus.js` records exactly that restoration. The page therefore marked a textual criticism, and a reading 通鑑 did not print, as 通鑑's words, while the key says 繁体字都是《资治通鉴》自己的话. **The gate passed it** because `isTongjianText` is a bare `corpusText.includes(s)`, and 「不可」 occurs inside two unrelated corpus 句. *Fixed*: the mark is gone and `[data-note="textual"]` notes are exempt from the requirement, with the exemption and its cost in the test header, the design of record and the proof.
2. **The figure gate is blind to every literal that is not `name: '…'` with a plain identifier key — reproduced.** The reviewer planted `altNote: "遂殺智伯，盡滅智氏之族。"` — a whole 句 in a double-quoted, undeclared field — in a copy of `src/figures/zj-split.js`, and the run stayed green; the file's own `quotations > 0` denominator was satisfied by the other fields. *Fixed*: all three literal forms are scanned, and the arm is kept as arm E in `gate-proofs.md`.
3. **The figure gate silently shrinks with its input — reproduced.** The module list was three hardcoded names filtered by `existsSync` with a `> 0` floor; renaming `zj-words.js` made the run report `2 module(s)` and pass, while the header claimed each of the three was checked. *Fixed*: the list comes from `src/figures/registry.js`, and the arm is kept as arm F.
4. **The header's headline claim is unbounded by the code.** The page half only reads runs the book wrapped in 「」, so an unbracketed quotation is invisible, and `isTongjianText` ignores script. *Fixed*: the header now states the predicate and the bound instead of claiming "no quotation goes unmarked".
5. **`QUOTED_FIELDS` overlaps what `test/lexicon.test.js` already covers** (its marked-run half matches whole `quote:`/`aside:` values in the module source). *Kept*, because the declaration is what makes a figure's data auditable field by field, and the overlap is the safe direction; the design doc's claim about a new figure failing the gate was true only for one quoting style and is now true for all three.
6. **The register entry must name a check for the class, not just the instance.** *Done*: the entry's table says **Nothing** for the false sentence about a chapter's own selection, and says why.
7. **Two figure-side asymmetries, judgement calls not breaks.** *No change*: a figure keeps one script rule (Traditional for 通鑑, Simplified for its own voice) and the round's rule-of-thumb holds inside it too; the module comments were re-read and are accurate after the `dukeNote` rewrite.
8. **The CSS rule, verified in a real Chromium rather than by reading.** On chapter 2 at 1440 px: 74 elements carry `lang="zh-Hant"`; exactly the 29 prose runs the rule should reach shrank and took `--ink-soft`; all 47 `.zj-pair__src` cells of the 原文 stayed at 20 px with no matching `font-size`/`color`; the card's five marked runs are laid out exactly as its own section says. Light-theme contrast unchanged, the four new colour pairs 6.9–7.2:1. **One push-back taken**: a marked run inside `.tb-margin-note` — the smallest prose in the book — was being dimmed and shrunk at once, to 12.4 px where the note is 13 px on a phone. The rule now floors the step at `0.8125rem`, so the mark never takes the book's smallest prose below its own UI size.
9. **「才」「德」「圣人」「小人」 are treated two ways, a page apart.** *No change, and the reason is the rule*: at 图 3.1 they are Simplifed and unquoted, which is the book naming its own subject; at the 思考 option they are quoted and marked, which is 通鑑's vocabulary being cited. That is exactly what the key states. The single-character bound is why 才 and 德 cannot carry the mark, and it is in the header.
10. **Header/code mismatches, no behavioural effect.** The header said "the four lines the corpus does not carry" while the list holds two. *Fixed*.

## What the review could not see

It read the diff and the rendered page; it did not measure whether a reader notices the mark (nothing does — stated in the proof), and it did not have the second book's reader's-eye view that produced the original complaint. Its own words on the class it found: the mis-marked variant is *"the same class as the 「不引原文」 sentence the change fixed, one step worse because the page now asserts it typographically."*
